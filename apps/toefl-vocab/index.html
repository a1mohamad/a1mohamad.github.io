01

Pick a book. Pick a day.

Two well-known lists come built in — 504 Absolutely Essential Words and 400 Must-Have Words for the TOEFL. They're already split into short daily sections, so you never face a wall of vocabulary.

Each section takes about ten minutes
A progress bar per book, so you can see the finish line
02

Be honest with yourself.

A word appears on its own. Tap the speaker to hear it. Then answer the only question that matters — did you know it, or not? No multiple choice to guess your way through.

The meaning appears right after you answer
Five little boxes track your last five tries
03

Your weak words come back first.

Open the same section tomorrow and it won't be in the same order. The words you keep getting wrong float to the top, and the ones you've nailed step aside. You don't have to organise anything.

Grammar tips appear with the definition where they help
Hear every word in a US, UK or Australian accent
04

See where you actually stand.

A single screen shows how much of the library you've mastered and which sections are still shaky. From there, one tap starts an Extra Practice drill built entirely from your weakest words.

The drill never disturbs your main progress
Export your progress and carry it to a new phone
