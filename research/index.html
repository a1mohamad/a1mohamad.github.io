How I approach machine learning

Experimentation-driven pipeline from raw data to rigorous evaluation.

Machine learning research is the science of extracting signal from noise. I begin with Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), statistical distributions, collinearity detection, and anomaly handling. A model is only as good as the data that feeds it.

I work across Computer Vision (TensorFlow/Keras) and NLP (PyTorch, Transformers) following a structured pipeline: dataset creation, preprocessing, feature engineering, cross‑validation, hyperparameter optimization, and residual diagnosis.

My research spans benchmarks like Titanic and California Housing to Kaggle challenges such as Cassava Leaf Disease and Quora Question Pairs. I build reproducible machine learning and deep learning systems with a strong focus on experimentation, evaluation, and model performance.
