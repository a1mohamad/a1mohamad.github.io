How I approach machine learning

A rigorous pipeline from raw data to production‑grade intelligence.

Machine learning research is more than just fitting models — it is the science of extracting signal from noise. My process begins with Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), where I investigate statistical distributions, identify collinearity, and handle anomalies. I believe a model is only as good as the data that feeds it.

Whether working on Computer Vision tasks using TensorFlow/Keras or Natural Language Processing with PyTorch and Transformers, I follow a rigorous pipeline: dataset creation, preprocessing, feature engineering, strategic cross-validation, hyperparameter optimization, and residual diagnosis.

My research spans from benchmark datasets like Titanic and California Housing to complex Kaggle challenges such as Cassava Leaf Disease and Quora Question Pairs. I also deploy models in production with FastAPI, Kafka, and maintain full MLOps pipelines with Airflow and MLflow.
