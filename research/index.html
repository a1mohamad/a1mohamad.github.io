Theoretical Foundations

How I approach machine learning

A rigorous pipeline from raw data to production‑grade intelligence.

Machine learning research is more than just fitting models — it is the science of extracting signal from noise. My process begins with Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), statistical distributions, collinearity detection, and anomaly handling.

I work on Computer Vision (TensorFlow/Keras) and NLP (PyTorch, Transformers) following a pipeline: dataset creation, preprocessing, feature engineering, cross‑validation, hyperparameter optimization, and residual diagnosis.

My research spans benchmarks like Titanic and California Housing to Kaggle challenges such as Cassava Leaf Disease and Quora Question Pairs. I deploy with FastAPI, Kafka, and maintain pipelines with Airflow & MLflow.
